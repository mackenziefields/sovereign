# Phase 4 real-data test guide

Goal: try the new PDF exports on your real coursework without touching your live Sovereign.

## 1. Make a limited backup
1. In Sovereign (your real browser), Dashboard, save a backup as you normally do. Leave it where it is.
2. Double-click `test-data/limited-backup.html` (it opens in a browser; nothing is uploaded).
3. Choose your backup, tick one course (best: one with at least two finished Demonstrate projects, one of them resubmitted, and a graded capstone), keep both boxes ticked, and save. You get `sovereign-test-copy.json`.

## 2. Open a separate copy of Sovereign
Use a **different browser, or a private window**, so it has its own empty storage. Open the Sovereign address, Dashboard, Import backup, choose `sovereign-test-copy.json`. Never import it into the browser that holds your real data: importing replaces what is there.

## 3. Re-attach a few files (optional)
Attached files are not in backups. To test them, open a finished project in the test copy and attach one image, one PDF, one Word (.docx) and one Excel (.xlsx) file, for example copies of the originals.

## 4. Export and check
Portfolio, Export as PDF. For each design (change it in the dialog) and for Letter and A4:
- Academic Record: grades, 80/20 course grade, assessment history, timeline wording (is anything stated that you know is untrue?).
- Professional Portfolio: no grades unless you tick the box; intro and closing text; order you chose.
- Tick one attached file at a time. Is what was left out clearly said?
- Click the contents entries in the saved PDF: do they jump to the right project?
- Feedback Report from a final review: findings, quotes, rubric, next steps.
- Read the small print: can you read every line on screen and printed?

## 5. Tell me
Anything wrong, confusing, ugly or misleading, with the course and design it happened in (no need to send the file unless you want to). Then delete the test copy and the limited backup.
